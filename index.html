Section 2: Math — Directions

Timing: 35 minutes are allotted for each module (22 questions per module).

Calculator: An acceptable calculator (including the built-in Desmos graphing calculator) may be used on all questions.

Reference Sheet: A formula reference sheet is provided via the "Reference" button at the top of the screen.

Question Formats: The test includes multiple-choice questions (with 4 answer options) and student-produced response questions.

Student-Produced Response Guidelines:

  • If an answer is a fraction like 7/2 or decimal like 3.5, you may enter either format.
  • Negative symbols must be placed first (e.g., -4).
  • Mixed numbers such as 3 1/2 are not permitted; enter 7/2 or 3.5 instead.

Important Information:
  • The number of degrees of arc in a circle is $360^\circ$.
  • The number of radians of arc in a circle is $2\pi$.
  • The sum of the measures in degrees of the angles of a triangle is $180^\circ$.
